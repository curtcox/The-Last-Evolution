Chapter 5: The Hydrogen Wall, the draft

The latest draft, acted and painted, 9:03 at 12 drawings a second, over the same sound as the storyboard. Following Round 5's notes, the hall is one continuous place: the camera pans and zooms from framing to framing in place of cuts, and Roal, the machines and the light move from mark to mark. The other spaces (his eyes, F-1's lens, the science plate, Morey's page, the rock) are reached by matched dissolves and pushes.

Notes: pause anywhere, type, and press Enter (or Pin) to pin a note to that moment. They stay in this browser. Copy notes puts them on the clipboard as text, timed and with their shot, for the decision sheet or a message.
