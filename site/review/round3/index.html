F-2, narrating

An ancient machine intelligence dictating its memoir, a hundred and twenty-five thousand years after the events. Male, mid-low, calm and unhurried, very precise diction, a dry understated wit, a faint weariness as if worn by time. Educated mid-Atlantic accent of the 1930s. Clear recording, close microphone.

What they read

I am the last of my type existing today in all the Solar System. I, too, am the last existing who, in memory, sees the struggle for this System, and in memory I am still close to the Center of Rulers, for mine was the ruling type then. But I will pass soon, and with me will pass the last of my kind, a poor inefficient type, but yet the creators of those who are now, and will be, long after I pass forever. So I am setting down my record on the menta-type. And so my task being done, I, F two, like Roal and Trest, shall follow the others of my kind into eternal oblivion, for my kind is now, as theirs was, poor and inefficient. Time has worn me, and oxidation attacked me, but they of Force are eternal, and omniscient. Roal — Trest — I was mistaken when I said no screen could stop that beam of Death. They had the screen, I have found it, too — but too late. These machines I have made myself. Two lives alone they can protect, for not even their power is sufficient for more.
