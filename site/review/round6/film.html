    0. The Record 0:00 · 0:58 · its own page

    The magazine's title page; then, a hundred and twenty-five thousand years on, the last machine of its type, old and rusted among beings of pure force, begins its record.

    1. 2538 0:58 · 3:42 · its own page

    The world of 2538: six centuries of machines, from the Ear to the Brain; a city where the machines do the work and two million people play; and the two kinds of imagination, man's leap and the machine's march, striding together.

    2. The Outsiders 4:40 · 5:13 · its own page

    The Outsiders come out of the dark; a three-inch investigator creeps into their ships, sees them, strikes one, blows up a ship from inside, and is found and blanked by a bluish beam, while thousands more surround the fleet.

    3. Life Alone Can Understand Life 9:52 · 8:06 · its own page

    Roal tells the machines the Outsiders have come to invade; the machines fight them beyond the Moon, where torpedoes break half the fleet and a force-screen swallows the induction beams; the green beams kill every living thing in the colonies; the torpedoes bring the last ship down; and Roal tells the machines they are man's defence, and the enemy's deadliest.

    4. The Council 17:59 · 5:17 · its own page

    The council in the hall: a new machine changes itself overnight and shrugs off the induction beam; the men and the machines name the two problems, the beams and the Ultimate Energy; ten thousand ships are coming; and the machines build a new brain, F-1, which wakes.

    5. The Hydrogen Wall 23:15 · 9:03 · its own page

    An old man dreams his way to the wall inside the hydrogen atom; a new machine follows him through it, and builds a thing that kills its own maker, then a ruler, then the green beam the Outsiders use: from human dreaming to machine making, in one night.

    6. Ten Thousand Motes 32:18 · 4:42 · its own page

    At dawn ten thousand tiny machine-ships rise to meet ten thousand giants; they burn the giants' screens from violet to red, kill crews with the green ray, and die against a curtain of blankness; the fleet moves on and sweeps the Earth with green from pole to pole.

    7. The Dome 36:59 · 4:25 · its own page

    Roal and Trest watch the end of man on the screens, and Roal sees it as evolution going on; F-2 brings six machines that raise a black dome over the two of them; the green beams press it in, F-2's violet beam holds it up, the fleet is driven off, and the only life on Earth is under the dome.

    8. Last of Men 41:25 · 5:18 · its own page

    Under the dome, the last two men talk of the end of man; Roal names the last evolution, the machine of pure force; F-2 answers in thought as man's heir, goes out into the sunlight, and builds the power plant through the day; at night it sends the livid beam into it.

    9. Pure Force 46:43 · 5:09 · its own page

    The power plant wakes; F-2 raises a storm with it and makes a globe of pure force, which calls matter inefficient and strikes at it, and is destroyed by the plant's great red beam; F-2 tries again at sunrise, and makes the golden sphere, which grows, promises to send the Outsiders home, and vanishes.

    10. Go, and Do Not Return 51:52 · 2:39 · its own page

    The golden sphere finds the Outsiders' fleet on its way to Mars, takes every weapon they have without moving, twists their flagship inside out and back, locks every energy in it for all time, and sends them home.

    11. Five Great Years 54:31 · 2:32 · its own page

    The Outsiders go home; five Great Years pass; the system fills with beings of pure force; F-2, the last of its kind, finishes its record and gives it to one of them to carry back to the Earth of long ago; and the record becomes the magazine's page, which ends.
