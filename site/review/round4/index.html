The look

Style frames for the plan's one rule: the world in ink wash on aged pulp paper, and colour only for energy, in the colours the text names. They test the medium, not the designs: the ships, machines and people are placeholders, drawn before their model sheets. Each still links to the full-size frame. The questions are in the decision sheet.
