## Proposal: a quality loop with a written finish line

PLAN.md §2 already says what I need before I sign off: the checks pass, I've watched the draft since the last change, and I can say why each beat works. The weakness is that those conditions live only in my judgment and in what I happen to remember. A loop built on that would either stop too early or never stop. So the mechanism has three parts: a written bar, a command that measures the work against it, and a hook that won't let me stop while something I can fix is still open.

### 1. `QUALITY.md`, the scorecard
This file lists every item that has to pass, with criteria concrete enough to check:
- **Film, per chapter:** the words match the script, the voice and picture stay in sync, figures act on their lines, settings hold still and only the energy boils, the lettering is legible and has no typos, codes scan, and the chapter sits at −14 LUFS.
- **Site, per page:** the content is right, the layout works on desktop and on a phone, links and anchors resolve, there's alt text and captions, and the page matches the current draft.

Each item records a status, a link to its evidence (a contact sheet, a frame strip, a screenshot, a check's output) and any concern I still have. An item passes only when its evidence is newer than the files it depends on. Change a scene and its "acting" and "sync" items go stale automatically.

### 2. `npm run quality`, the measurement
This command runs everything that already exists: verbatim, links, refs, boil_check, loudness and the speech-to-text voice check. I'd add the checks that are missing:
- a staleness check for annotated.html;
- caption timing against the word alignment;
- black or blank frames;
- decoding QR codes from rendered frames;
- phone-width screenshots of every page;
- a check of the live site after each deploy.

Then it compares input hashes against evidence dates and prints a queue of open items. Each item is tagged as mine to fix or as **Curt's call**: voices, look direction, the foreword's wording, and anything already settled in DECISIONS.md. The command exits 0 only when nothing on my side is open.

### 3. A Stop hook, the engine
While a flag file (`.quality-loop`) exists, a Stop hook runs `npm run quality` each time I try to end a turn. If items of mine are still open, it blocks the stop and hands me the top item. Each pass of the loop:
1. **Look first.** Render frames or contact sheets, listen through hear.py, or screenshot the page.
2. **Fix it,** then re-run the checks.
3. **Record the evidence** in QUALITY.md, then commit.

Deleting the flag turns the loop off, so ordinary conversation isn't affected.

### Guarding against my own blind spots
- **Fresh-eyes rounds.** Every few items, a subagent with no context reviews a chapter or page cold against the rubric, and its findings join the queue. Outside models via `npm run ask` can do the same within the $10 cap; spend is about $0.59 so far.
- **When it stops.** The loop stops when one of these holds:
  - the queue is empty and two fresh-eyes rounds in a row find nothing above minor;
  - only Curt's calls remain;
  - I hit a budget limit: model spend, ElevenLabs credits, or an iteration cap per session;
  - one item fails three times, in which case it goes to Curt rather than round and round.
